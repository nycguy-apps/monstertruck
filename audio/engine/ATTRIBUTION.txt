Engine audio model attribution

Rumble Roam's engine is procedural audio generated by a cross-plane V8 combustion,
intake and exhaust simulation. No recorded engine sample is included in this
release.

Simulator: engine-sound
Author: pbonnard
Source: https://github.com/pbonnard/engine-sound
License: MIT (upstream README and package metadata identify the project as MIT)
The associated JavaScript controller, WebAssembly binary and AudioWorklet are
included under the MIT license; see src/audio/v8-engine-sim/LICENSE.txt.
The simulator's built-in impulse response is synthetic. No proprietary or
personal recordings are used.
